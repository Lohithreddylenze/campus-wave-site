Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

CampusWave is a school management app made by Off-Look Innovations LLP, Bengaluru, India, operating under the brand Aasarion ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the CampusWave app, the CampusWave web app and this website (campus-wave.com) collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

In short: schools use CampusWave to run their day. The school decides which student, parent and staff information goes into the app, and we process it only to provide the service to that school. We do not sell personal information, and we do not show advertising.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Schools that subscribe to CampusWave (the "school" or "customer").
  • Users the school gives a login to: management, teachers, office staff, students and parents or guardians.
  • Visitors to this website, including anyone who books a demo.

For information about students, parents and staff, the school is the data fiduciary (the organisation that decides why and how the data is used) and we act as its data processor, following the school's instructions and our agreement with the school. If you are a parent, student or staff member with a question about your information, please contact your school first; we will help the school respond.

2. Information we collect

Information the school provides

  • Student records: name, class and section, admission number, date of birth, gender, photograph, parent or guardian names and phone numbers, address, transport route, and academic information such as attendance, homework, marks, results and remarks.
  • Staff records: name, role, subjects and classes, phone number, email, employee ID and attendance.
  • School records: classes, subjects, timetables, fee structures, fee payments and receipts, notices, events, exam schedules and certificates.

Information created while using the app

  • Activity in the app, such as attendance marked, homework posted, notices sent, fees recorded and reports generated, along with who did it and when.
  • Messages and notices sent through the app to parents, students or staff.
  • Content you upload, such as notes, lesson resources, photographs and documents.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical information: device type, operating system, app version, language, IP address, crash reports and diagnostic logs.
  • Usage information: which screens are opened and when, used to keep the service reliable and to improve it.

Information from this website

  • When you book a demo, the details you enter (name, school name, phone number, city and role) are sent to us through WhatsApp or email. This website itself does not set tracking cookies.

3. How we use information

  • To provide CampusWave to the school: logins, attendance, homework, exams, fees, transport, notices, reports and the other modules the school uses.
  • To send notifications the school chooses to send, by app notification, SMS, WhatsApp or email, such as attendance alerts, homework, fee reminders, bus alerts and notices.
  • To generate drafts with AI features, such as question papers, lesson plans and timetables, which staff review and approve. The school's syllabus, question bank and timetable constraints are used for this; student personal information is not used to train AI models.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse, fix problems and improve performance.
  • To support the school and respond to requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Children's information

CampusWave holds information about students, many of whom are under 18. We process it only on the instruction of the school, which obtains any consent required from parents or guardians under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We do not use children's information for advertising, profiling for marketing, or tracking across other apps or websites. Students sign in with credentials issued by their school.

5. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only:

  • Within the school, according to each user's role: for example, a teacher sees their classes, a parent sees their own children, and management sees the whole school.
  • With service providers who help us run CampusWave, such as cloud hosting, SMS, WhatsApp and email delivery, push notifications, payment processing and crash reporting. They may process information only to provide their service to us.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users, the school or us.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, in which case this policy continues to apply.

6. Where information is stored and how it is protected

Information is stored on secure cloud infrastructure, with data for schools in India hosted in India wherever available. We protect it with encryption in transit, access controls based on role, separate logins for every user, regular backups and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure, so we also ask schools and users to keep their login details private and to tell us immediately at the contact below if they suspect misuse.

7. How long we keep information

We keep school information for as long as the school subscribes to CampusWave, and for a short period afterwards so the school can export its records, after which it is deleted or anonymised unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Schools can ask us to delete specific records at any time. Diagnostic logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted.

8. Your rights and choices

  • You can view and correct much of your own information in the app; for anything else, ask your school.
  • Parents, students and staff can ask their school to access, correct or delete their information, or to stop a particular type of notification. We will act on the school's instructions.
  • You can turn off push notifications in your device settings. Notifications the school sends by SMS or WhatsApp are controlled by the school.
  • You can ask us to delete the account and data linked to it by writing to info@campus-wave.com from the email or phone number registered with the school.

9. Third-party links and services

The app and this website may link to other services, such as the Google Play Store, the Apple App Store, WhatsApp and LinkedIn. Their own privacy policies apply to what you do there.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CampusWave changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell schools through the app or by email.
