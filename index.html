      Attendance

      A paper register, counted by hand and carried to the office.

      Marked on the teacher’s phone. Parents of absent students hear about it the same morning.

      Homework

      Written on the board. Parents find out at the next meeting.

      Posted to the class with a due date. Students and parents are notified straight away.

      Exams and results

      Papers typed from scratch, marks in a spreadsheet, report cards filled in one by one.

      Papers drafted by AI, marks entered once, report cards generated for the whole class.

        Do we need to buy new hardware?

        No. CampusWave runs on the phones, tablets, computers and classroom smart boards your school already has. Teachers, students and parents use the mobile app, and the school office uses the desktop software.

        Can parents and students use it on their phones?

        Yes. Everyone gets their own login on the mobile app, so a parent sees their child’s attendance, homework, results and notices, and a student sees their timetable, tasks and exam dates.

        Can it be customised for our school?

        Yes. Classes, subjects, fee structures, exam patterns, documents and branding are set up to match how your school already works, and we keep adapting as you grow.

        What happens to our existing records?

        Share your student, staff and class lists in our templates and our team loads them for you before go-live.

        Will you train our staff?

        Yes. We train office staff and teachers during setup, and our support team stays available after go-live.

        Are there extra charges for features or updates?

        No. Every module is included, and new features arrive at no extra cost.
