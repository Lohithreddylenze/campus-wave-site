Team ID cards

Every Aasarion team member who visits a school carries an ID card with a QR code. Scanning it opens that person’s verified profile on campus-wave.com, so a school can confirm who they are talking to.

1Scan the cardPoint your phone camera at the QR code on the card.
2Check the IDThe profile shows the same ID number as the card. If they don’t match, call us.
3Save the contactOne tap saves the person’s number and email to your phone, or opens WhatsApp.

Profiles are only reachable by scanning a card or through a link shared by the team member; they are not listed here.
